About JKUAT IEC

A student-led hub for creative minds, builders, and visionaries at JKUAT.

Our Mission

We are dedicated to fostering a culture of innovation and entrepreneurship among students. Our goal is to empower the next generation of leaders by providing a platform where raw ideas transform into impactful, real-world solutions.

Whether you're developing software, building hardware, or launching a startup, our community provides the resources, network, and guidance you need to succeed.

What We Do

Hackathons

We host innovation challenges where students build real solutions under pressure and in teams.

Projects

Members collaborate on software, hardware, and startup ideas from concept to launch.

Mentorship

We connect students with industry professionals, alumni, and experienced entrepreneurs.
