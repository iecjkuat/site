Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, register for events, or contact support. This includes your name, email, student registration number, and course details.

2. Use of Information

We use your information to operate and improve our platform, send you notifications about club activities, and facilitate event management.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with club leadership for administrative purposes or with the University if required by policy/law.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can manage your preferences in the Settings page.
